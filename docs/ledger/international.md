# International football and confederation cups

> Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-09-25 to keep session context small. This is the detailed ledger for this area: read it before touching the code it describes, and add new entries here, not to CLAUDE.md. References to "this file" below meant CLAUDE.md as a whole; the other sections now live alongside this one in `docs/ledger/`.

**International football** (`core/international/`) — national teams, played entirely inside the offseason
- **Four-year cycle. Watch-only unless the user manages one of the nations** (see "National team management" above, which is layered on top of everything in this section rather than changing any of it) (`INTL_CYCLE_YEARS`=4, `qualifyingLeg`/`isTournamentSeason`). Keyed off `season % 4`: seasons 1/2/3 of a cycle each play one qualifying leg, season 4 (`% 4 == 0`) plays the `INTL_FIELD_SIZE` (**32**) team `World Cup` (8 groups of 4 → `INTL_KO_SIZE` (16) bracket, R16→QF→SF→Final reusing the Continental Cup's `seedOrder`/`resolveCupTie`). **Qualifying is ONE campaign drawn once (at the cycle's first qualifying offseason, `qualifyingLeg==0`) and played a leg at a time across the three offseasons** — the campaign persists on `LeagueStore.international.qualifying`, accumulating results, and its 32 qualifiers are computed (`computeQualified`) only when the third leg completes; the tournament offseason then draws the World Cup from them. Each leg is seeded independently off the campaign's start season + leg index (`playQualifyingRound`), so leg-by-leg play is deterministic. Squad pids are filtered to living players each leg (a 3-season campaign outlives some players named at draw — `leagueMatchData` would deref a retired pid otherwise). Every nation but the user's auto-picks (`selectSquad`: best 23, ≤3 GKs, fit players before injured; `chooseBestFormation`). `LeagueStore.international` (`{qualifying, tournament, history, qualifyingHistory, powerRankings, stage, stageInjuries}`).
- **The field is 32, and the binding constraint is nationality depth, not club count (2026-08-23, user call).** Raised from 16 once the world reached 16 competitions. Measured on a fresh 320-club world, stable across 3 seeds: 78 nationalities present, **44** clear `INTL_MIN_POOL`, and squad rating runs ~76 at #1, ~70 at #16, **~64 at #32**, then falls off a cliff to ~56 by #40 — so 32 is near the largest field this world fills with nations worth watching, and 40 would be padded. **The accepted cost is that qualifying means much less:** at 16 places, 28 of the 44 could miss out; at 32, only **12** can, and all of them are European or African. A confederation with no more nations than places plays *no qualifying matches at all* (`initQualifying` skips it), so South America (4 eligible), Asia (1) and North America (1) now qualify their whole membership — Asia and NA already did at 16. **Do not try to widen the field by lowering `INTL_MIN_POOL`**: dropping it to 15 adds ~5 nations (Mexico 16, Gambia 16, Kenya 15, Albania 15) that all rate below 56. The only real fix for the qualifying stakes is more league countries feeding deeper nationality pools.
- **The World Cup's size is a per-save setting now, and the "32" above is only its default (`LeagueStore.worldCupSize`, 2026-09-11, user report).** 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 or `"auto"`, chosen on `/new-league` and changeable on National Teams → Qualifying (NOT God Mode: turning God Mode on un-fogs every potential in the save, too high a price for a tournament format, and the setting can't reach a campaign in progress anyway). Shapes live in `WORLD_CUP_FORMATS` (`international/format.ts`); 24 and 48 are the Euro 2016 / 2026 World Cup formats, top two plus the best third-placed sides. Four things to know. **(a) A qualifying campaign records `fieldSize` AND `places` at the draw, and `planQualifying` reads them back rather than recomputing.** A campaign spans three offseasons, so recomputing at the last leg would re-deal places its groups were drawn for if the setting or the allocation rule changed in between. A campaign without them (drawn before this) replays the old rule exactly: 32 places, strength-weighted, floor of one. **(b) Absent means 32, not the `"auto"` new saves start on**, because Auto on a pre-626-club world (44 eligible) resolves to 24 and would quietly shrink an existing dynasty's World Cup. No migration. **(c) The eligible count is a MOVING target, which is why Auto's bars are a table (`WORLD_CUP_AUTO_MIN_NATIONS` 16/36/48/96) and not a ratio.** A default world fields 66 at generation, then 74-82 as youth intake tops thin nationalities past `INTL_MIN_POOL` (seeds 1-3, 8-11 seasons, still creeping on seed 3). Ratios putting 48's bar at 80 and then 87 were both tried and both left a mature default save a handful of nations from flipping its World Cup mid-dynasty; at 96 a default save plays 32 for life. **(d) Best-third seeding (`seedWithBestThirds`, `simIntl.ts`) pairs on group-stage record, not partner groups**: best winners v the thirds (weakest third to the best winner), the other winners v the weakest runners-up, the strongest runners-up v each other. Zero first-round rematches is guaranteed; keeping a group's top two in opposite halves is best-effort (they still share a half 32% of the time at 24, 25% at 48, over 20 draws). 16 and 32 take the old partner-group path byte for byte, and `bestThirdsFor` derives the count from the group count, so every confederation cup shape reads 0 and is untouched.
- **Qualifying places are dealt by REAL FIFA QUOTAS, not by strength (`allocateByQuota`, `CONFEDERATION_QUOTAS`, 2026-09-11, user call).** The old rule (`allocateSlots`) weighted each confederation by how many of the world's strongest N nations it held, with a floor of one. A real save's North America (nine weak nations, no contender) therefore got ONE place, and since `groupCountFor` never makes more groups than places, all nine were drawn into one group played three times: 24 games each for one spot. The user asked for it to work like the real thing. Now: (1) weights are the real allocation for a World Cup of that size — 1974 for 16, 1986 for 24, the 1998-2022 quotas for 32 (host place left out), 2026 for 48 (its intercontinental playoff shared a third a side); (2) caps: no confederation sends more than `QUOTA_MAX_SHARE_OF_ENTRANTS` (2/3, CONMEBOL's real share, the highest any has) of its entrants, raised one place at a time for whoever sends the smallest share when the caps can't fill the field; (3) floors: one place per `INTL_QUAL_GROUP_MAX` (7) nations, so no group outgrows seven; (4) the rest by Sainte-Laguë on the quota weights. **Strength plays no part, deliberately**, and a test pins that reversing which nations are strong moves nothing. **Three things to know.** (a) It DOES change a default world's qualifying (unlike the rest of this PR): measured at 32, Europe 18 → 16 of 31 and Asia 2 → 4 of 6, everything else equal, which is the real 1998-2022 shape. Only international results move; the club-side channel is injuries carried out of international matches, the same one the 16 → 32 field change accepted without an audit. (b) **The floor was first one place per FIVE nations and it swamped the quotas at small sizes** — at 16 it handed Africa 4 of 16 against a real quota of 1 — so it became one per seven, which only bites where a quota is genuinely too small for its confederation. (c) `allocateSlots` stays, unchanged, because a campaign drawn before campaigns recorded their own `places` replays it (see `planQualifying`).
- **`finishOf` counted knockout rounds FORWARDS from a three-round bracket and was wrong from the day the field went to 32** — a round-of-16 exit read "Quarter-finals" and a quarter-final exit "Semi-finals", inflating History's semi-final counts. It now names the finish by rounds from the final (sound only because an archived tournament is finished) and `FINISH_ORDER` gained "Round of 32"/"Round of 16". Past tournaments read correctly with nothing to migrate, since it is derived.
- **`seedBracket` splits the draw into halves, and that was a real bug fix, not tidying (2026-08-23).** It used to emit each partner pair's two ties *adjacently* (`W(a) v R(b)`, `W(b) v R(a)`, then the next pair), which puts them together in the **next** round — so a group's two qualifiers met in the semi-final of a four-group tournament, and would have met in the quarter-final of an eight-group one, contradicting the function's own docstring. Mirror ties now go into a second accumulator appended after the first, which is also how a real bracket is laid out. **It changes results for existing four-group tournaments too** (the World Cup's old shape and the confederation cups' largest) — harmless because international matches take no draw from the shared `rng`, so no club result moves. Pinned by a test that asserts group-mates land in opposite halves at 2/4/8 groups.
- **The knockout is ONE repeating `"knockout"` stage, not one stage per round.** `IntlStage`'s `"qf" | "sf" | "final"` collapsed into it when the bracket gained a round, because bracket depth is a function of the field (4 rounds at 32, 3 at 16, and a small confederation cup's can be a single final) and hard-coding it means a new stage every time the field moves. `roundsRemaining` (`tournament.ts`, shared with the confederation cups for the same reason) reads the depth off the fixtures, and that is what names the round in the Dashboard button/headline and in Schedule's round picker — **never the stage name and never `KO_ROUND_NAMES` indexed directly**, or an old save's quarter-finals get labelled "Round of 16". `migrate.ts`'s `migrateIntlStage` maps the three old values onto it; an in-progress bracket therefore finishes at whatever size it was drawn.

- **Played in stages the user clicks through, *before* the offseason advances (`core/international/staging.ts`).** The campaign is *drawn* the instant the club season ends (in `simThrough`, at the offseason transition — `initInternationalCampaign` names squads + draws groups/bracket, fixtures unplayed) and then *played* one stage at a time (`playIntlStage`): a qualifying offseason plays this cycle's one qualifying leg; a tournament offseason is group stage → QF → SF → Final. (A qualifying campaign is drawn only in the cycle's *first* qualifying offseason; the next two resume the in-progress campaign for their leg — see the four-year-cycle bullet above.) `InternationalState.stage` (`"qualifying"|"groups"|"qf"|"sf"|"final"|"done"|null`) tracks progress; the Dashboard's "Advance" button is replaced by the stage buttons (`isIntlStagePending`) until it reaches `"done"`. `simThroughInternational` plays every remaining stage at once (the "sim through" button, and the headless-test path). **The gate is presentational, not structural** — `simOffseason` *opens* by calling `simThroughInternational`, so it always completes a campaign the user left unplayed. That's what the Dashboard's **"Skip qualifying"/"Skip the World Cup"** button relies on: it just routes to `/set-scouting` (the normal advance), plays nothing itself, and lands identical results because the stages own their seeds (equivalence test in `international.test.ts`). **Each stage owns its seed** (`GROUP_STREAM`, `KNOCKOUT_STREAM + round`), so click-through == one-pass == old bulk result — `runQualifying`/`runTournament` are thin composers over the very same stage functions (equivalence test in `international.test.ts`). Init is player-free and shared-rng-free; every match runs on its own `hashInts` stream (`QUALIFYING/GROUP/KNOCKOUT/DRAW_STREAM` tags), **never the shared `rng`**, so club results stay bit-identical (`touchStats` hash + `simThrough` gate unchanged).
- **Now runs entirely *before* `simOffseason`, so retirement falls *after* the tournament** — a player about to retire gets one last campaign, and (as before) one injured in May still misses it, because squads read end-of-season ratings/injuries. Caps/goals/assists are credited per stage (additive `CareerDelta`); the one-off `tournaments`/`titles` counters and the `history` summary land at the Final. `simOffseason`'s old step 1.7 is gone.
- **A nation = a synthetic club.** `nationMatchData` builds `LeagueTeam`s keyed by `nid` (index into the campaign's `squads`, stands in for `tid`) and pools the whole field into **one** `leagueMatchData` normalization baseline — mandatory, exactly like `cupMatchData`, or z-norm flattens every nation to parity. `simMatchDetailed` runs unmodified.
- **Eligibility + fieldability.** A nation enters if pool ≥ `INTL_MIN_POOL` (18) with ≥ `INTL_MIN_KEEPERS` (1) GK **and** has a confederation (`confederationOf`). The GK floor is a *correctness* requirement: `selectXI` fills a GK slot with an outfielder when none exists, corrupting the keeping composite. **44 of the 78 nationalities present clear the bar on a fresh 320-club world** — measured, identical on 3 seeds (`scripts/intlFieldProbe.ts`).
- **Confederations + slot allocation** (`confederations.ts`) — `CONFEDERATION_OF` maps every name-pool nation to one of 6 confederations (test-guarded: `international.test.ts` asserts no nation is missing). `allocateSlots` distributes the 16 places by largest-remainder **weighted by each confederation's count of *contender* nations** (the global strongest 16), floor 1, capped at nation count. Weighting by raw nation *count* was measurably wrong — Africa's 8 nations matched Europe's 24 for places while Europe held every strong side, filling the field with makeweights.
- **Qualifying is a three-leg round-robin** (`INTL_QUAL_LEGS`=3, `roundRobin(nids, legs)`), **one leg per qualifying offseason** — each fixture is tagged with its `leg` (0/1/2) at draw time (`IntlGroupMatch.leg`, optional for legacy saves → treated as 0), and `playQualifyingRound` plays the next unplayed leg via `playGroups(..., onlyLeg)`. A single round-robin left strong nations missing out on variance; three legs across three offseasons make a long, low-variance campaign that reads like real World Cup qualifying. Tournament groups stay single-leg (variance is the point). Serpentine group draw for qualifying (`serpentineGroups`), pot draw for the tournament (`potDraw`). `splitPlaces` walks group finishing positions so a confederation always fills its allocation: every position that takes every group's place is through outright, and **the one position the places run out in goes to a playoff** between all its nations (`qualifyingPlayoff.ts`, 2026-09-12, user ask — it used to be "the N best" on per-game points). `rankAcrossGroups` now only seeds it (who hosts, who meets whom). The bracket (`runPlayoff`) is built so nobody qualifies without winning a tie: while the pool is ≤ 2× the places left every winner is through and the losers play on; above that, the bottom of the pool plays elimination ties down to exactly 2×. That matters because the shipped world's commonest shape is **6 for 4** (Europe at 32 and 48), where a plain bracket would need byes handed out on points. Played inside the final leg's stage (no new `IntlStage`), single-leg at the better seed with ET/pens via `resolveCupTie`, on its own stream (`QUALIFYING_PLAYOFF_STREAM` 850 + round, per confederation index), box scores dropped like every qualifier. Stored as optional `IntlQualifyingCampaign.playoffs` / `IntlQualifyingSummary.playoffs` (no migration; absent = decided on points), folded into `nationForm` at qualifying weight, and `qualifyingPlan().playoff` drives the page sentence and zone bars so they cannot drift from the rule. Applies to any campaign whose last leg is still to play. `scripts/qualifyingPlayoffShapes.ts` prints the shapes a world produces.
- **Career record + injury carry-over; no rating/finance impact.** `Player.intl` (`IntlCareer`: caps/goals/assists/tournaments/titles, in `international/career.ts` to avoid an import cycle with `players/types.ts`) accumulated at sim time (`CareerDelta`), surfaced on Player Profile. **Nothing feeds progression or valuation** — cannot disturb the rating equilibrium. **Injuries do carry into the new club season** (`stageInjuries`, `collectInjured`, `carryIntlInjuries`): a knock in an international match sidelines the player for the opening weeks of the club season. Collected *post-hoc* from box-score injury events (never stamped mid-campaign → international match XIs/results are unchanged), held on `InternationalState.stageInjuries`, applied in `simOffseason` (step 3.05) **after** progression heals the club-season knocks, rolled on a dedicated seeded stream (`INTL_INJURY_STREAM`, never the shared `rng`). The rolled duration is reduced by `INTL_INJURY_OFFSEASON_RECOVERY` (2) — the summer break serves part of the recovery, so a short knock heals before kickoff and only a more serious injury carries, at a shortened spell. Note this is the *one* intended club-side effect: it changes club availability the following season, so multi-season league results after an international campaign are no longer bit-identical to a pre-feature baseline (single-season `touchStats` hash is untouched — `simSeason` never crosses an offseason). **Fatigue and value/hype bumps remain deferred** (would touch the equilibrium; need their own audit). Migrated: new arrays/`stageInjuries` backfill to empty, `Player.intl` absent = never capped.
- **"Light" archival + the National Teams section.** Only the *current* qualifying campaign and tournament are held in full; past editions collapse to compact self-contained summaries — **results, not box scores.** `IntlTournamentSummary` now carries final group tables (`IntlGroupTable`, nation-keyed via `groupTableSummary`) + every knockout scoreline (`IntlKnockoutResult`); `IntlQualifyingSummary` carries the qualifying group tables + qualifiers; both drop per-match box scores and squad lists (career totals on `Player.intl` are the lasting per-player record). `InternationalState` gains `qualifyingHistory`, `powerRankings` (an `IntlPowerSnapshot` — every eligible nation ranked by squad rating, taken each time a campaign is drawn in `initInternationalCampaign`), plus the existing tournament `history`. Archival happens at sim time: qualifying summary appended when qualifying finishes, tournament summary at the Final. Per-nation records (titles/finals/best-finish) are **derived**, not stored (`nationHistory.ts` `nationRecords`/`finishOf`). Old saves backfill the new arrays to empty and fill going forward (migrate.ts). UI: the **`/national-teams/*`** section (sidebar group, ordered *after* Team; `ui/pages/nationalTeams/`) — World Cup, Qualifying, **Rosters**, Schedule, Power Rankings, Stat Leaders (national teams + players filterable by country, off `Player.intl`), History. Navigation is sidebar-only (the pages carry no tab strip of their own). The old single `/international` page is gone (redirects to `/national-teams/world-cup`).
- **Rosters tab + per-campaign player record.** `Rosters.tsx` renders the live campaign's `NationSquad[]` — nation list sorted by squad rating, selected squad sorted by `sortByPosThenOvr` with the projected XI (`selectXI` off the squad's own formation) shaded. Squad pids missing from `league.players` are dropped, matching what `playQualifyingRound` feeds the sim (a 3-offseason campaign outlives some named players). **`IntlCareer.seasons`** (`IntlSeasonLine[]`, `career.ts`) breaks caps/goals/assists out per offseason played, `kind: "qualifying" | "tournament"` — surfaced as the Player Profile's third stats tab. Written by `applyCareerDelta`, which therefore takes `season`/`kind`; `season` is **threaded in from the caller** (`playIntlStage`/`simThroughInternational` gained a param) rather than read off the campaign, because a qualifying campaign's own `season` is the cycle's *start*, not the offseason being played. The squads-only call at the Final passes an empty delta and must not stamp empty rows — `mergeSeasonLine` only runs for players with a delta entry. Old saves backfill `seasons: []` (irreconstructable — archived campaigns hold no box scores); career totals stay the authoritative record. "Which campaign is current" is one shared helper (`liveCampaign` in `nationalTeams/shared.tsx`), used by both Rosters and Schedule: pending stage first, else **the more recent of tournament/qualifying by `season`** — "tournament if there is one" was stale, since a newly drawn cycle's qualifying outranks the previous World Cup. **Render volume is the qualifying schedule's gotcha:** a campaign is ~280 fixtures (39 nations, 7 groups, `INTL_QUAL_LEGS` legs), so Schedule filters to one round at a time by default (`currentLegOf` mirrors `playQualifyingRound`'s next-leg rule; "All rounds" opts back in), and `NationName` passes `tip={false}` to `Flag` — the tooltip only repeats the adjacent name and costs an absolutely-positioned shadowed span per flag (1674 → 186 flag nodes on that page).

**Confederation cups** (`core/international/confederationCup.ts`, `format.ts`) — the Euro / Copa América / AFCON, played inside the offseason alongside a World Cup qualifying leg
- **Cadence is additive, not a restructure.** They run in the offseason of the cycle's *middle* qualifying season (`isConfederationCupSeason`, `CONFEDERATION_CUP_QUALIFYING_LEG` = 1, i.e. `season % 4 == 2`), which is the real Euro/Copa offset from a World Cup. That offseason plays its qualifying leg **and then** the championships. Replacing the leg instead was rejected: it would drop `INTL_QUAL_LEGS` 3 → 2 and reopen exactly the qualifying variance those three legs exist to damp.
- **No confederation cup qualifying.** A field is the confederation's strongest nations at draw time (`buildSquads` is already sorted). A second qualifying campaign per confederation would multiply the ~280-fixture campaign qualifying already runs.
- **The format scales with the field, and it has to** (`format.ts` `TOURNAMENT_FORMATS`/`formatFor`): 16 → 4 groups of 4, 12 → 4 of 3, 8 → 2 of 4, 6 → 2 of 3, ≤5 → **one round-robin whose top two contest a final** (which is how Copa América was really played until 1975, so it reads as a format rather than a fudge). Every shape ends in a power-of-two knockout. `seedBracket` therefore takes its group count from `groups.length` and a `qualifyPerGroup` param instead of `INTL_GROUPS`/`INTL_QUALIFY_PER_GROUP`; a single-group tournament returns `[winner, runnerUp]` since there is no partner group to cross with. World Cup output is byte-identical (defaults preserved).
- **Measured world support is the reason three of six ship dark.** Every player is generated from one of the eight European leagues' nationality tables, so non-European nations exist only as imports. On a fresh world (seed 1): Europe **25** eligible nations, Africa **12**, South America **5**, North America **1**, Asia **1**, Oceania **0** (`INTL_MIN_POOL` 18 + ≥1 GK). So the Euro, AFCON and a small Copa are held; the Asian Cup, Gold Cup and OFC Nations Cup are defined, gated on `CONTINENTAL_MIN_NATIONS` (4), and light up on their own if a world ever supports them. **Don't "fix" this by lowering `INTL_MIN_POOL`** — that changes who enters World Cup qualifying, hence the confederation allocation, hence existing saves' results; and a pool under ~14 can't field 11 fit players, which `selectXI` does not guard.
- **They are played side by side and aligned on their finals.** One `"confederation-groups"` stage plays every championship's groups; each `"confederation-ko"` stage plays a round only in the tournaments whose `roundsRemaining` equals the deepest still running, so a 1-round Copa idles through the Euro's QF and SF and every final lands on the same click. Click count therefore matches a World Cup's. That alignment is also what lets the archive/credit step assume **all champions are decided on the same stage** (`playIntlStage`'s `"confederation-ko"` case credits squads + titles and archives in one pass) — break the alignment and that assumption breaks with it.
- **Each tournament owns its rng streams**, keyed by `confederationIndex` (draw 900+i, groups 920+i, knockout 940+i*10, +round). Sharing one would make each championship's results depend on the order the others were played in. Nothing touches the shared `rng`, so club results stay bit-identical; `playTournamentGroups`/`playTournamentRound`/`playKnockoutRound` gained an options/`streamBase` param for this and default to the World Cup's tags.
- **`IntlCareer.confederationCups`/`confederationCupTitles` are separate counters, deliberately.** `tournaments`/`titles` have meant World Cups since they shipped and are already ranked on by Frivolities' International board and `goat.ts`; folding a second, easier trophy into them would silently restate every existing all-time record. `IntlSeasonLine.kind` gains `"confederation"` for the same reason — see the awards note below.
- **Ballon d'Or credit (2026-08-19, user call).** `WORLD_AWARD_INTL_CONTINENTAL_MULTIPLIER` (1.5, between qualifying's 1 and a World Cup's 2) and `WORLD_AWARD_CONTINENTAL_BONUS` (0.7, half `WORLD_AWARD_WORLD_CUP_BONUS`) feed the existing **international** component rather than a new one. Sized on the same `POTY_GOAL_WEIGHT.FWD` exchange rate as the rest of the team-achievement block: ≈9 goals — second-smallest of the five, above only the domestic cup's ≈3 — because a confederation cup field is a *subset* of the world and its winner is already being paid for that summer's qualifying caps. Applied uniformly whatever the field size — scaling by entrants would punish a Copa winner twice. Re-run `scripts/worldAwardsAudit.ts` (winner's world-ovr rank) before touching either; the whole block trades against that rank and raising these alongside the others cancels.
- **Bug this exposed, and it was latent rather than new:** `intlComponent` read `seasons.find(l => l.season === season)` — the *first* line for a season. A confederation cup offseason gives a player **two** (its qualifying leg and the championship), so one would have been silently dropped. It now sums every line for the season. Pinned by a test.
- **State + UI.** `InternationalState.confederationCups: IntlConfederationCup[]` (live, replaced only when a new edition is drawn, so the last one stays browsable) and `confederationCupHistory: IntlTournamentSummary[]` (same Light summary a World Cup collapses to, plus `confederation`). Kept out of `history`/`nationHistory` so the World Cup record keeps meaning what it did. New `/national-teams/confederation-cups` page shows every championship of one summer on one page; `KnockoutColumns` takes `totalRounds` so a 2-round bracket's round 0 reads "Semi-finals", not "Quarter-finals"; `liveCampaign` treats a confederation cup stage as *not* a World Cup stage (it runs in a qualifying offseason, so Rosters/Schedule should keep showing the live qualifying campaign). Migrated to `[]`/0 — past seasons played none, so the blank is exact rather than a guess.
- **Watch on any future change:** save size (three more live tournaments hold box scores until the next edition) and injury load (that offseason now plays a leg *and* a tournament, so more knocks carry into the club season via `stageInjuries`).
