## Milestone status (per `SOCCER_GM_SPEC.md` §7)

All done and merged:
- **M0** — engine port.
- **M1** — players → composites: generation, per-position OVR, composite rollup + league normalization.
- **M2** — season loop, IndexedDB persistence, web worker, scheduling, `simThrough`, minimal UI.
- **M3** — box scores: per-player attribution, `simMatchDetailed`, `SeasonStats`, box-score/leaders pages.
- **M4** — dynasty mechanics: progression (`players/progression.ts`), aging, retirement, youth intake, free agency, contracts, offseason orchestrator (`offseason.ts`).
- **M5** — match texture: cards, fatigue + AI subs, corners, penalties, in-match injuries (multi-matchday recovery), stoppage time, composite re-rolls after subs/reds/injuries. Sub selection blends fatigue with a live `computeMatchRating` estimate (`subPriority`) to pick who comes *off*; a **bench-quality gate** (`worthSub`/`tiredValue`/`benchValue` in `matchSim.ts`, `SUB_FRESHNESS_BONUS`/`SUB_QUALITY_MARGIN`/`SUB_FATIGUE_RELIEF`) decides whether the swap is worth it at all — a fresh replacement may fall short of the tired starter's ovr by an allowance that grows with the starter's energy deficit, so weak benches hold their tireds on (holds back ~1 sub in 10; rebased the `touchStats.test.ts` scoreline hash, intended). **`SUB_QUALITY_MARGIN` was retuned 1→2.5 (2026-07-23)** — at 1 the gate held back ~1 sub in 5, twice the intended rate, which cost the champion enough late-game quality to push the M1 standings gate (`simThrough.test.ts`, champion 78-94 pts) below its floor on main. Measured over 15 seeded seasons: margin 1 → 78.7 champion pts, 2.5 → 80.8, always-sub → 79.8. Note the gate's own 5-season sample is noisy (single seasons swing 70-87), so it sits near its floor by construction — **re-measure over ≥15 seasons before reading a small move in it as a regression.** The gate is also **form-aware** (`SUB_GATE_RATING_INFLUENCE`, `liveRatingFor`): the tired starter's live `computeMatchRating` (vs the 6.0 baseline) shifts `tiredValue` — a starter playing well is harder to justify hooking, one playing poorly easier — reusing the same signal `subPriority` uses for the who-comes-*off* pick. The form term is mean-zero around baseline, so it redistributes *which* subs are held without moving the ~1-in-10 aggregate; rebased the scoreline hash a second time (intended). `MatchPlayer` now carries `ovr` for this. See **Roster & tactics** for the user "more minutes" flag. **The two-checkpoint schedule this shipped with is GONE (2026-09-08)** — subs now happen in windows and `tiredValue` has been split into `gateValueOf` plus an explicit `subAllowance`, which is the same arithmetic in play and a different one at half-time; see the substitution-windows entry under **Match data & stats** before touching any `SUB_*` constant.
